just us — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

just us is a private journal shared between two people. This policy describes what information the app handles and how.

What we collect

  • Email address — used solely to create and sign in to your account. Never used for marketing, never shared.
  • Content you create — photos, notes, emojis, and moods you place on your canvases. Visible only to you and the one partner you explicitly invite to your journal.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No use of your content for anything other than showing it to you and your partner.

Where your data lives

Content is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, protected by row-level security so only the two members of a journal can access its content. Photos are stored privately and served through short-lived signed URLs. A copy of your recent canvases is cached on your device so the app works offline.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account. Content in the shared journal remains available to your partner, since it belongs to both of you. If both members delete their accounts, contact us to have the journal's remaining data removed, or delete items from canvases before deleting your accounts.
