just us — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

just us is a private journal shared between two people. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we handle

  • Account information — your email address and an internal account identifier are used to create and secure your account. If you choose password sign-in, the credential is handled by Supabase Auth; we do not receive your password in plain text.
  • Profile and shared-journal information — optional display names, your journal name, anniversary date, appearance settings, membership, and invite information.
  • Content you choose to add — photos, videos, voice recordings, notes, emojis, moods, page titles, and the layout and styling of your canvases.
  • Service and notification information — record identifiers, content creation and update times, recent app-activity and notification times, and an Apple Push Notification service device token. We use these details to sync changes, support offline edits, and avoid sending unnecessary notifications.

The app accesses only the photos and videos you choose through Apple's system picker. It uses the microphone only when you choose to record a voice note. The app does not request your device's live location and currently does not store location metadata from selected media.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you and maintain your account.
  • Create, display, sync, and back up your shared journal.
  • Let you and your invited partner collaborate.
  • Send requested service notifications.
  • Maintain security and diagnose operational failures.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

Who can see or process information

Content in a journal is available to its two members. Do not add anything you do not want your partner to see.

We use the following service providers to operate the app:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, file storage, synchronization, and server functions.
  • Apple provides app distribution, the system photo picker, and push-notification delivery.

These providers may process network and device information, such as IP addresses and request logs, as needed to deliver and secure their services. They process information under their own privacy terms.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising, third-party tracking, or analytics SDKs, and we do not use journal content for marketing or advertising. We may disclose information if required by law or when necessary to protect users, the service, or others.

Storage and security

Remote data is stored with Supabase. App access is protected by authentication and row-level database rules intended to restrict each journal to its members. Media is stored in a private bucket and delivered using time-limited signed URLs.

Recent journal data and pending offline changes may be stored on your device. The app clears its local journal cache when you sign out, leave a journal, or delete your account. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information while your account is active and keep shared journal content until it is deleted or the journal is removed.

You can delete individual journal items in the app. You can delete your account through Settings → Delete Account. Account deletion removes your account, membership, notification tokens, and local app cache. Content already added to the shared journal remains available to your partner because it is shared content; its authorship link is removed. Delete journal items before deleting your account if you also want those items removed.

If both members have deleted their accounts, or if you need help removing remaining shared-journal data, contact us using the address below.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
